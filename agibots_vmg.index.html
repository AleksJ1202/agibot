04 — Limitations & decisions

What needs to be resolved first

The operational model matters as much as the robot capability itself.

01 / CONTROL

Who controls the robots?

Define operating ownership, remote-control authority, escalation and who intervenes when a robot cannot complete a task.

02 / POWER

Charging & availability

Confirm charging locations, cycle time, battery endurance, overnight charging and how many robots remain operational at once.

03 / ENVIRONMENT

Can they operate outside?

Validate weather resistance, temperature range, ramps, uneven ground, crowds, thresholds and outdoor navigation reliability.

04 / WORKFORCE

Supplement, not replacement

If robots require constant support, supervision or rescue, they may become an additional burden rather than a workforce multiplier.

Operating principle Robots should absorb repetitive, low-risk and information-heavy tasks while VMG retains judgement, accountability, approvals and intervention authority.
